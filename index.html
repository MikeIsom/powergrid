Power Grid viewer bundle

Built viewer for Boardgamers' Power Grid. Paste one of these into BGS's Custom Viewer URL:

Standard (loads its stylesheet from beside the script):
https://mikeisom.github.io/powergrid/powergrid-viewer.umd.min.js

Inline CSS (styles baked into the script):
https://mikeisom.github.io/powergrid/inline/powergrid-viewer.umd.min.js

Built from viewer commit 41a9c2b on top of boardgamers/powergrid master (engine 2.0.37, viewer 2.3.32).
